Branch database/arcs-and-agendas. Text only; no model field, no save break, no RNG draw. `Flavor/Arcs.cs` (engine), `ArcsModern.cs` (24 arcs), `ArcsOrigins.cs` (7), `OwnerAgendas.cs` (25 owners), `OwnerVoices.cs`. Hooks in `WorldTick.AdvanceWeek`: `Arcs.RunWeekly` just before `Digest.Weekly`; `OwnerVoices.RunWeekly` at the VERY END (the monthly block and the Origins timeline post news dated the same day; a pass before them never reads it, because the next window starts after that day). BiddingWars' "signs with ... after a bidding war" now passes `f.Id` as AboutFighter (the owner voice filters on it).

Arcs: cast by "First Last" (NOT FullName: it carries the nickname). Beats share a Slot = alternatives; a slot needs the one before it told. Conditions are observable. Title beats need a COMPLETED TITLE BOUT in the window (winner/loser as the text says): Modern never sets `Vacated`; a retiring champion's belt passes to the heir (`Lifecycle.VacateBelt`, no fight) and a leaving champion's reign just ends (`ContractLifecycle.LeavePromotion`): neither is "dethroned"/"loses". A defence needs the reign to have started BEFORE the event date. Bout conditions look back 28 days AND only past the previous beat's date; cap 4 beats/week, start index rotates by week (capacity, not rotation, is what keeps a dozen simultaneous beats safe). Day-one calendar already books some pairs, so those slot-1 beats fire in week one.
Origins arcs never open on Booked (CRU announces an empty card; garage pairs on the day): they open on Won / WonStreak / SignedWith.
Fired record = accolade on the Lead "Saga: {arc}: {beat}" (never evicted; news caps at 1000). `Arcs.IsSagaMark` filters them out of the two Wpf honours lists (Rows.cs AccoladesText + achievement rows). Arc ids/titles/tags are the saved record: never rename.
Text rules (tests enforce): a beat is told whenever its condition comes true, so the text may only say what the condition proves: no digits, no house/city/division/camp/age (use {Belt} {AOrg} {BOrg} {AWhere} {BWhere} {ARec} {BRec}; a LeftOrg beat may name its pinned house), no first/only/never/every/nobody/ever, no tonight/today/finally, no hidden-ability words; Origins: no ranking/stream/belt/title/glove. "Is now {AWhere}" not "left for a bigger stage".
Owners: stateless. Agenda keyed by (designed house abbreviation, owner name, mode): a house founded later with a random owner can never pick one up. Quotes are sex-neutral (tests render for a man and a woman) and use First Last for {F} (FullName has the nickname in straight quotes). Signing = " signs with " only (a renewal is not a signing), " away from " = signs + outbid, dethrones/claims = crowns, vacates/retirement = loses a star. Quote is posted dated the SOURCE item's date. Raczkowski (Fenwick) is a camp coach, no agenda.
Test lessons: never `Assert.Contains(bigCollection, predicate)` on w.News or the arc tables (xunit formats the whole collection on failure: 7+ GB, hung the run); use `Assert.True(x.Any(...), msg)`.
Left out (needs save break): former teammates (camp history), per-arc progress counters, stored feud reasons. Wire-worthy agendas for world-sim: BuryRival (Ilic vs Reuss, Bittencourt vs RCS), Sell (Feldstein), Hometown (Nwosu, Zulu, Chambi, Wattana), Spectacle (Faisal, Ravelo), Money (Hennessy, Cartwright), Legacy (Kanemura).
